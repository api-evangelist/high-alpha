# High Alpha

> High Alpha is a venture firm based in Indianapolis, IN. We create and fund B2B SaaS companies — partnering with founders to build businesses that reach their full potential. We're early-stage investors, and fund companies across the United States.

## Investment in Early Stage B2B SaaS Companies
- [Investment Types](/capital): High Alpha invests in pre-seed to Series B B2B SaaS companies. Many times, the firm will back an anchor investment in a Series A financing, leading the round and taking a board seat. High Alpha also invests in Seed, Series A, and Series B rounds alongside trusted investors, which we call co-investing. 
A detailed overview of the different funding rounds and capital types we offer.
- [Studio Starts](/studio): In addition to investing in early-stage B2B SaaS companies, High Alpha starts companies with founders through its Studio. The Studio offers founders services in design, brand, marketing, HR, recruiting, product design, and go-to-market to help founders go faster.
